# Lore draft: the lands below the Bell (run 3, for Clarence's approval)

Status: **APPROVED by Clarence, 11 Nov 2026.** Shown in the game (LORE_APPROVED = true in src/rules/data.ts).
Source: The Unwritten design log: Lanternfall's funeral bell rings beneath a sealed shrine; the dead rise with their names taken;
the Hollow Bishop erases the names of the dead with the stolen bell; Blackroot; the death realms (Ashen Hells, Hollow Sea, Winter
Sepulchre, Bleeding Court, Astral Wastes); the Ashborn (fire and curse, death-realm knowledge). The escapes already use the death realms
themselves, so these lands are the **living world under the Bell**, sinking toward them: each one borrows the feel of a realm without
being it. All names are original.

The idea in one line: *below the Bell, the wall between the living and the dead grows thin, and each land is closer to a death realm.*

## Gimmick bosses, events and weekly modifiers (plain names, no new lore)
- Gimmicks: **Shield** ("The boss raises a ward: your taps barely scratch it for 10 s. Your Named and pets still hit."), **Tolling** ("The boss tolls: your Named stop. Tap the bell to stop it.").
- Events: **Eclipse** (crit chance up), **Bell Toll** (2x gold for 30 s), **Grave Rush** (5 treasure foes in a row).
- Weekly modifiers: Fire week, Frost week, Storm week, Venom week (+15% damage of that element).
