## Description (replace the old one)

**Lanternfall's dead have lost their names.**

Tap to fight. Win back the names of the dead and they fight beside you. When you stall, fall: wake in a realm of death, reach its gate before the clock runs out, and return stronger. Or carry a scar forever.

**What's inside**
- 5 races and 5 classes, each class with its own way to fight (Rage, spells, weak spots, arrows, Prayer), tiered subclasses (T1–T7) and ultimates
- Transformations earned through quests (Vampire, Werewolf, Draconic, Undead, Elemental), hybrids, or stay Mortal Master
- Elements and immunities; every boss has a weakness
- Class gear sets, legendaries and Artifacts that change how your class plays, plus 26 named items hidden across 13 lands
- Dungeons, realm sets with rule-breaking bonuses, 100 relics, 60 pets, 10 Named allies
- A 100-step quest chain, daily tasks and achievements
- Hand-made pixel art; plays in your phone's browser and saves on your device

Free to play, no ads. If you enjoy it, a tip helps the next update.

## Devlog

**Title:** 2.5: What You Carry

**Type:** Major update

**Body:**

2.5 is about gear. Your gear now changes how your class plays, not just how big your numbers are.

**Class gear**
Every class has 3 sets (weapons, accessories, armour), 2 legendaries and 1 Artifact, all built around its own loop:
- **Warrior:** Rage Strikes set off a Fury that lifts all your damage.
- **Mage:** every cast surges your damage for a while.
- **Rogue:** your combo goes deeper, and every point adds damage.
- **Ranger:** arrows fly harder while they fire on their own.
- **Cleric:** Prayer climbs higher and lifts your Named further.

They drop in dungeons. Gear made for another class still gives its stats, just not its effect.

**The Stash**
Don't throw a set away because you've moved on. Put it in the Stash (Gear > Stash). Each complete set there, up to 10, gives +2% of your worn gear's stats and a small form of its bonus.

**Found: 26 named items**
Every land hides 2 named items that its bosses drop now and then. Track them under Gear > Found. Finding 5, 13 and all 26 gives gems.

**Vigil: the Cleric keeps watch**
While you're away, a Cleric keeps climbing towards 90% of their best stage (a full climb takes 8 hours) and comes back with full Prayer. It works after a Fall too.

**Judge gear by**
On the Bag tab (or in Settings), pick what matters to you: Balanced, Tap damage, Named damage, or Gold and boss time. The bag arrows, Compare and auto-salvage all follow your choice. Compare also tells you when a piece completes a set.

**Smaller changes**
- Quest 2 now teaches your class (Rage Strikes, spells, weak spots, foes or Prayer) instead of asking for 500 taps.
- When your race or faith rule kicks in, a ⚑ note tells you. Hero > Stats counts how often it worked this life.
- A boss that runs out your clock now says so clearly, with a tip on what to do next.
- Finished quests are claimed from the quest bar in one tap.
- Fixed: the Toll Collector offer could show through other popups and couldn't be tapped.

Your save carries over. The full notes are in the game under Menu > Patch notes.
