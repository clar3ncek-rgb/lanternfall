## Description (paste into the Description box)

**Lanternfall's dead have lost their names.**

Tap to fight. Win back the names of the dead, and they fight beside you. When you stall, fall: wake in a realm of death, race its gate before the clock runs out, and return stronger. Or carry a scar forever.

**What's inside**
- 5 races, 5 classes with AdventureQuest-style tiers: base class, then three subclasses each from T2 to T7, with an ultimate at the top.
- Transformations earned through quests (Vampire, Werewolf, Draconic, Undead, Elemental), with hybrids, or stay **Mortal Master** to the end.
- Diablo-style elements and immunities: every boss has a weakness.
- Dungeons with realm sets and rule-breaking set bonuses, 100 relics, 60 pets, 10 Named allies, a 100-step quest chain, daily tasks and achievements.
- Hand-made pixel art. Plays in your phone's browser; your game saves on your device.

Free to play, no ads. If you enjoy it, a tip helps the next update.

*A spin-off of* The Unwritten*, a game still in design.*

## Before you press Publish
- Open the page on your phone and play for a minute (character creation, a few taps, a tab or two).
- Saves live in the browser on each device. "Menu → Move your save" makes a code to carry a save between devices.
