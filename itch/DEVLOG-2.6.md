## Devlog

**Title:** 2.6: Heavier Steps

**Type:** Major update

**Body:**

2.6 slows the game down so you can see what's happening. Thanks to everyone who said it was a blur of one-tap kills.

**Foes stand longer**
Normal foes have 4x the health, eased in over the first 12 stages, so each fight takes a few taps instead of one. A stage now has 4 foes instead of 8, and each one pays more gold and XP, so you climb about as fast as before. Bosses, escapes and dungeons are unchanged. Kill quests ask for half as many.

**A calmer start**
Fewer things at once:
- **Tabs open one at a time:** Quests at stage 5, Skills at 15, Gear at 25 and Death at 40. Pets open with your first egg. A Mage gets Skills with its first class point. A locked tab shows the stage it opens at.
- **Items come slower:** bosses drop items 15% of the time, and every 10th boss for sure, starting at stage 25. Normal foes now and then drop a Common item. Elites, treasure foes and wanderers drop 5% of the time.
- **Every drop counts:** every item that reaches your bag is announced. Drops worse than what you wear are still salvaged for gold.
- **Points come slower:** the first 30 quests give fewer class and transformation points, and race levels come more slowly.

**Race trees reworked**
Each race tree now has one damage node, and every other node does its own job:
- **Human:** more Marks, and a chance to skip ahead a stage.
- **Elf:** more treasure foes and fairies.
- **Orc:** Bloodcry finishes off normal foes at low health.
- **Troll:** more time in dungeons.

Points you already spent stay where they are.

**Easier on a phone**
- Character creation scrolls on every phone, ticks your current pick, and ends on the Faith step (type your name in the box at the top).
- Holding a button to read its details no longer selects the text.
- The tap counter on the fight now says "streak".
- New, shorter class descriptions that name all three subclasses.
- Hints now say which kind of point is waiting, and what still hurts a foe that's immune.
- Clearer labels: the Named portraits under the fight show their level, the button beside them says how many more you can hire, and the Hero tab tells your XP level apart from Training.

**Fixes**
- The stage dots under a foe no longer keep growing while you farm a stage.
- An escape gate could be far too tough to finish in time. It now checks your real damage once and shrinks if it was clearly impossible. You still have to fight for it.

Your save carries over. The full notes are in the game under Menu > Patch notes.
