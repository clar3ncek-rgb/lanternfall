# Parked: gems (pay to win), for later

Clarence, 11 Nov 2026: "i want to introduce some form of pay to win in the future like gems". Not built; noted so later work fits it.

## What already fits
- Escapes are now **adaptive** (run 3): each escape foe is sized from the damage you really deal in that escape, so raw power
  cannot make an escape easy. Gems should therefore buy **escape help** rather than damage there, for example extra escape seconds,
  a second chance after a failed escape (no scar), or a rare (special) escape.
- Outside escapes, power still matters, so gems could also buy boosts there (gold or damage for a time, extra keys, eggs, Forge levels).

## Facts to settle before building
- Today the game is free on itch.io with optional tips, and has no server. Saves live on the phone and the save code is an unsigned
  checksum (run 2 dropped a "supporter code" for that reason). Anything sold could be copied or edited locally; real purchases need a
  payment provider and a server that grants and checks gems.
- itch.io sells whole games, add-ons and tips, not in-game currency inside a browser game; selling gems would mean another payment
  route (and its rules, fees and refunds), or a store app.
- Your own rule for money: a paid service needs your yes first.

## Questions for when you pick this up
1. What gems buy (escape help, boosts, cosmetics, or all).
2. Where they are sold (itch add-on packs, a web shop, an app store) and whether gems can also be earned slowly in play.
3. Whether this stays a hobby project or becomes a paid product (it changes privacy, tax and support duties).
